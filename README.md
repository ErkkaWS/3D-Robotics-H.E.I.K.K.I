<h1>3D-Robotics Course Project: H.E.I.K.K.I -
<br> Haaga-Heliaa Edustava Intellektuelli Kaljaa Kittaava Individuaali</h1>
<h3>Approximate translation follows: 
<br>The Haaga-Helia Representative Intellectual Beer-Guzzling Individual</h3>

Greetings and welcome!
<br>We builded this monstrosity thingymajig using 3D printed stuffelings, Robotic thingelings by using Arduino IDE.

H.E.I.K.K.I is an ESP32-powered animatronic unit with a singular purpose: TO OBTAIN BEER.
Equipped with a 10-LED thirst bar, two servos (head and arm), an electrode sensor, and I2S audio output, H.E.I.K.K.I monitors his own hydration levels in real time.
As thirst builds, the status bar fills up. When critically dehydrated, he raises his head, pounds the table and demands a cold one by name.
If given a drink via the electrode sensor, he slurps it down, raises his arm in celebration and rewards the generous donor with a hearty burp.
If denied, he plays a deeply disappointed sound effect, hangs his head in defeat, and snores until next time.

> No professors were harmed in the making nor naming of this project.
